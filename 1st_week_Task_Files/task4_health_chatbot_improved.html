What causes a sore throat?
🩺
A sore throat is most commonly caused by:
  • Viral infections — cold, flu, or COVID-19
  • Bacterial infections — strep throat (Group A Strep)
  • Dry air or irritants like smoke
  • Acid reflux (GERD) irritating the throat
  • Allergies and post-nasal drip

Most sore throats resolve within 7 days. If you have high fever or difficulty swallowing, seek medical attention.
⚠️ Consult a doctor if symptoms persist beyond a week.

Is paracetamol safe for children?
🩺
Paracetamol can be safe for children when dosed correctly:
  • Dose is based on body weight (10–15 mg/kg per dose)
  • Can be given every 4–6 hours, max 5 doses in 24 hrs
  • Always use child-formulated syrup or drops
  • Avoid adult tablets without medical guidance

Overdose can cause serious liver damage — always follow the dosage chart on the packaging.
⚠️ Always consult a paediatrician before medicating a child.
